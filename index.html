Beyond the résumé

More about me.

Origin

I was born in Ukraine and grew up between two languages before adding a third. Cleveland, Ohio is home now.

I taught myself to code through small projects and a lot of failed attempts. Somewhere in there I burned out for close to a year and stopped writing code entirely. What brought me back was an internship at Cleveland Clinic, where a model I'd trained ended up in front of surgeons. That's when AI stopped being a subject and became the thing I build.

How I work

I build retrieval from scratch before I reach for a framework, because I want to know what's underneath. I'd rather publish the eval that caught a 90% train/test leak in my own work than a demo that hides it.

Along the way I've co-founded a startup, shipped inside a hospital's security team, and run Discord communities for the products I've worked on. Each one taught me something a course couldn't.

What I believe

Shipping isn't finishing. Shipping is proving something works once.

Off the keyboard
